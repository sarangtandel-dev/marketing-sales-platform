# Client intake is a human-led form

A person on our team completes the intake form with the Client. The Client does not fill it in alone. Answers from intake become `client-stated` Facts. A person asking the questions can probe vague answers and flag High-risk Claims as they come up, which a self-serve form cannot.

## Milestone

M1. Client #0 intake is informal in M0. See ADR-0039.
