# Version 1 launches with dental clinic, urgent home services and professional/B2B service packs

The three version 1 Category Packs are:

- **Dental clinic**: local, appointments, regulated claims.
- **Urgent home services**: local, service area, call-first.
- **Professional/B2B service**: not local, consultation or demo request.

On-site purchase is out of scope for version 1.

## Considered options

- **Furniture store (previous choice):** dropped. Its product catalogue needs prices and stock we can't publish without verified, constantly refreshed data. It would also have been the third local pack, when we need one non-local pack to prove local features really are optional.
- **Online business / e-commerce:** deferred. It means building checkout or linking to one. A later pack will link to a checkout the Client already owns.

## Milestone

M1: professional/B2B pack. M2: dental and urgent home services. See ADR-0039.
