# Client intake form

Send before the kickoff call. Everything here is needed by the playbook; an incomplete form delays go-live.

## Business

- Legal entity name, brand name, GST or registration number (for Meta verification)
- Registered address, service area (cities, radius)
- Primary phone (display), WhatsApp number to use for business (must not already be on the WhatsApp app), owner mobile for alerts
- Business hours and timezone
- Google account to own everything (create one: `growth@{{domain}}` recommended)
- Primary email for tool sign-ups (same as above)

## Domain and email

- Existing domain? Registrar login or authorisation to transfer
- Existing website? URL, platform, is anything worth keeping (URLs with traffic, content)
- Business mailbox: none / Zoho / Google Workspace / other

## Services and offer

- Top 3-5 services in priority order, with one paragraph each and typical price range
- One primary conversion: quote / booking / call / WhatsApp
- Any current offer or seasonal promotion
- 3 customer testimonials with first name and area, photos of 5 recent jobs
- Top 5 questions customers ask before buying, with the owner's answers (feeds nurture emails)

## Channels

- Google Business Profile: exists? manager access
- Social accounts: Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube (URLs)
- Directory listings: JustDial, IndiaMART, Sulekha, others
- Paid ads: running now? platform, monthly spend, who manages
- Slack: yes / no (else alerts via WhatsApp to the owner)
- Scheduler: wants meeting reminders? (then Cal.com)

## Compliance

- Serve EU customers? (cookie banner wording)
- Consent to appoint the agency as processor and to the privacy policy template
- Lawyer to review privacy policy? contact

## Brand assets

- Logo (SVG or PNG on transparent), brand colours, fonts if any
- Photos: team, premises, work (minimum 15, landscape, 1600 px wide or more)

## Sign-off

- Who approves the site before launch, and their turnaround commitment (48 h recommended)
- Who receives leads (name, email, mobile), who is the HubSpot second seat
